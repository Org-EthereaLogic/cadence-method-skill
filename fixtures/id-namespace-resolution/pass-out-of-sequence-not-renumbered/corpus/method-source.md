## Requirements

This section lands its work packages out of numeric order, the pattern the
project's own authority document uses (WP 1.5 landing after WP 3.1); none
of the identifiers below is reused or renumbered by that ordering.

**FR-5** The later-landing work package's requirement, defined first in this text.

**FR-2** The earlier-numbered work package's requirement, defined second in this text.
